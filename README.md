# Flip Clock Widget (Android)

A home-screen clock widget in the classic flip-card style: big hour and minute cards with a split line, the day and date underneath, and your next alarm.

- Tap the clock: opens your alarms
- Tap the date: opens your calendar
- Follows your phone's 12/24-hour setting
- Resizable
- No permissions, no internet, no battery drain (Android ticks the clock itself)

## Install

1. On your phone, open https://github.com/ajone79/flip-clock-widget/releases/latest
2. Tap `FlipClock.apk` to download it
3. Open the downloaded file
4. If asked, tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", go back
5. Tap Install
6. Open the Flip Clock app
7. Tap "Add widget to home screen"
8. Tap Add

## Add the widget by hand

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen
2. Tap Widgets
3. Find Flip Clock
4. Drag it onto the home screen
5. Long-press the widget and drag the edges to resize

## Update

1. Download the newest `FlipClock.apk` from the releases page
2. Install it over the old one (the widget stays where it is)

## Build

Every push to `main` runs the tests, builds the APK and publishes it as a new release.
